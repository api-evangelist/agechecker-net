# AgeChecker.Net

> AgeChecker.Net is an online age-verification service for e-commerce merchants selling age-restricted products. It verifies customers during checkout using identity-record matching, phone verification, government-issued photo ID review, configurable selfie-with-ID workflows, and other optional verification steps. More than 90% of customers are typically verified instantly using submitted identity and checkout information, while photo ID is commonly used when automatic verification is unsuccessful or when required by a merchant's configured workflow.

AgeChecker.Net also offers an ID barcode scanning and validation solution for retail merchants, enabling in-store age verification at the point of sale by scanning a customer's government-issued ID barcode.

AgeChecker.Net integrates with all major e-commerce platforms including Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento, Lightspeed, and more. Merchants configure age requirements by geography (country, state, county, city, or ZIP code) using a built-in workflow editor with preset rule templates for tobacco, alcohol, ammunition, and other regulated industries.

Verification methods include: instant database lookup against public records and identity networks, SMS phone validation, government-issued photo ID upload with AI-assisted OCR, selfie + ID matching, and electronic signature capture. Customers verified on one merchant's site are recognized across the network, reducing friction for repeat buyers.

The service is used by businesses selling tobacco, alcohol, firearms accessories, ammunition, CBD, adult content, and other age-restricted goods, as well as by companies that need to verify the identity of an individual — such as financial institutions processing loan applications, rental services, and other organizations with identity or age confirmation requirements.
